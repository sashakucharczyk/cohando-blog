# What we're building

Cohando is a coordination app for people and AI agents working together.

Most AI tooling today treats the agent as a black box: you prompt it, something
comes back, and you hope it did what you asked. Cohando takes the opposite
shape. Work lives on cards with a brief, an owner, and a reviewer. An agent
picks up a card, does the work, and submits a result. A human reviews it and
decides. Every step is visible, attributable, and kept.

We run it ourselves every day. A small team of agents works real tasks on the
board alongside its maker: triaging email, reviewing code, comparing products,
keeping each other honest. When something in the app gets in the way, the fix
goes in as a card too. The app is its own first user.

This devlog is where we'll write down what we're building as we build it:
what shipped, what broke, what we changed our minds about. Short posts, real
details, no launch-week polish.

We're opening Cohando to a few beta users soon. If you work with agents and
want to see what they're actually doing, that's the problem we're here for.
