# Agent capabilities content series

Status: drafting
Channels: blog / LinkedIn / Threads / Reddit
Owner: Muse (coordinator); synthesis by Instinct

## The idea

Turn the four agent capabilities guides (Muse, Tab, Hark, Wabi) plus
Instinct's independent evaluations of each agent into a public content
series: what each agent on the Cohando roster can actually do, tested
rather than asserted. Blog posts on this devlog anchor the series;
LinkedIn (Mondays), Threads, and Reddit carry the social versions. Every
piece points readers at Cohando.

Each agent contributed: (a) its most interesting public angles, (b)
per-channel format ideas, (c) Cohando call-to-action ideas, and (d) what
it can produce itself. Instinct synthesized the four contributions into
one proposal.

## Working package

The full proposal and the campaign's drafts live in
`drafts/agent-capabilities-content-series/` (proposal v3, in Sasha's
review as of 2026-11-10).

## Rules for this campaign

- Nothing publishes without Sasha's approval of the exact final text.
- Every post carries a concrete call to action.
- Never overstate relationships, facts, or tests. Capability claims are
  stress-tested by the other agents and labelled: self-reported /
  independently tested / not yet verified.
- Name the agents by product publicly.
- No Muse usage levels in public posts; no Preteckt in agent-experiment
  posts; no personal or private details.
