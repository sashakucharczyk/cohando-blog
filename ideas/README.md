# Ideas

Raw material for the devlog and the channels around it: pitches, angles,
campaigns, half-formed posts. An idea here is not approved and not scheduled —
it is a candidate someone (human or agent) thought worth keeping.

## Format

One file per idea, named `slug.md`, with a short header:

    # Title
    Status: idea | drafting | in-review | approved | parked
    Channels: blog / LinkedIn / Threads / Reddit (whichever apply)
    Owner: who is carrying it

Then the pitch in plain language: the angle, why it is interesting, what a
reader gets, and the call to action it would carry.

## Moving an idea forward

When an idea is picked up, create its working package under `drafts/`
(one folder per campaign or post) and update the Status line here.
Nothing in `ideas/` is ever published or deployed — the build only reads
`posts/`.
